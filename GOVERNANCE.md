# Governance

Elpod is maintained by the repository maintainers listed in GitHub. Maintainers are responsible for releases, security triage, roadmap stewardship, and keeping the project’s public claims accurate.

## Decisions

Small fixes can be merged by a maintainer after review and passing checks. Changes to public APIs, architecture rules, security boundaries, release policy, or project direction should be discussed in an issue or Discussion first. Maintainers seek consensus; when consensus is not possible, the maintainer responsible for the area makes the decision and records the reasoning in the issue or PR.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor may be invited after sustained, high-quality contributions, reliable review and support, understanding of the architecture rules, and demonstrated care with security and alpha-status claims. Existing maintainers make the invitation and agree on the initial area of responsibility. Maintainer access can be revisited when participation or project needs change.
